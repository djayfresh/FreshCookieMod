Fresh Cookies (FreshCAA) - Cookies Are Amazing

Vanilla gives you one cookie. One. Fresh Cookies fixes that.

Back in 2014 this mod added five cookies to Minecraft 1.6.4 and then took a very long nap. It is now awake, rewritten from scratch for NeoForge on Minecraft 26.3, and it brought the whole bakery with it.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Punch grass. Along with wheat seeds you will now find Peanuts and Grapes. Plant them on farmland like wheat.
2. Find a Pecan or Macadamia tree in a forest, plain or savanna. Break the leaves for nuts and saplings.
3. Craft the Sun Drying Table (sticks in the corners, gold on the edges, a diamond in the middle) and put it somewhere the sun can reach it. Grapes in, raisins out.
4. Egg + Sugar + Wheat gives cookie dough. Mix it with cocoa, raisins and oats (oats come from wheat), peanuts, pecans or macadamias, then bake it in a furnace.
5. Eat cookies.

THE COOKIES
Chocolate Chip, Oatmeal Raisin, Peanut Butter, Pecan and White Macadamia. Raw dough is edible. Raw dough is also a gamble.

THE SUN DRYING TABLE
Grapes go on the mesh. Sunlight does the rest. No fuel, no redstone, just a table sitting in the sun turning grapes into raisins while you watch them shrivel. Rain, night or a roof stop it, because that is how sun works.
Slot in up to four Lenses (iron nuggets around a glass pane) and each one hovers over its side of the table, focusing a glowing beam of light onto the grapes. Four lenses dry five times faster. The beams are very pretty. Please do not stare into them.

CROPS AND TREES
Peanuts and grapes drop from grass and ferns in place of wheat seeds (the odds are in the config) and plant on farmland like wheat. Pecan and Macadamia trees grow in forests, plains and savannas, drop nuts and saplings, and their logs make planks.

THE FACTORY WORKER
Hard hat, earmuffs, safety glasses. Wanders around in the sun looking for the nearest chest. Nobody knows who hired him. He has never once been seen carrying a cookie.

ALSO
- A Fresh Cookies creative tab
- Seven advancements, from Getting Started to Macadamia Dance
- Config file at config/freshcaa-common.toml: lens speed bonus, grass drop weights, dough poison chance
- Works on servers and in singleplayer

- NeoForge 26.3.0.8-beta or newer
